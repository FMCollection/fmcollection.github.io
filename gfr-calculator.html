GFR (Glomerular Filtration Rate)

GFR estimates how well your kidneys filter waste from your blood, based on age, gender, and creatinine level. This is a genuine clinical measure — but a concerning number here is not a diagnosis. Real GFR readings come from a lab-verified blood test interpreted by a doctor, not a self-entered estimate.
