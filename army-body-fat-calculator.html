U.S. Army body fat standard

This method estimates body fat from neck, waist (and for women, hip) circumference rather than skinfold calipers or a scan. It's the same style of estimate used in U.S. military fitness assessments — quick and equipment-free, though less precise than a DEXA scan or hydrostatic weighing.
