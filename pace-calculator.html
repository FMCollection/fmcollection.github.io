Minutes per kilometer

Pace calculator

Pace is simply time divided by distance, shown as minutes per kilometer. It's the standard way runners compare speed across different distances, so a 5K pace and a marathon pace mean the same thing side by side.

Once you know your pace per kilometer, it's easy to plan splits for longer efforts — for example, if your pace is 6:00/km, a 10K at even effort should land close to 60 minutes. To convert pace to speed, divide 60 by your pace-in-minutes: a 6:00/km pace works out to 10 km/h. Most runners find pace more useful than speed for race planning, since it maps directly onto how race distances and split times are usually discussed.

Pace — common questions

How do I convert my pace to km/h?

Divide 60 by your pace in minutes per km. A 5:00/km pace, for example, works out to 12 km/h (60 ÷ 5).

What's considered a good beginner running pace?

There's no universal 'good' pace — it depends entirely on your current fitness. Most beginners land somewhere between 6:30 and 8:30 per km for easy-effort runs, and pace naturally improves with consistent training.
