Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What this site is

VitalCalc (fmcollection.github.io) is a free collection of health and fitness calculators. This policy explains what information is and isn't collected when you use the site, and how third-party services we use — Google Analytics and Google AdSense — handle data.

Calculator data: nothing is collected

Every number you enter into a calculator — your height, weight, dates, or any other field — is processed entirely inside your own browser using JavaScript. None of it is sent to, stored on, or logged by any server. Closing or refreshing the page erases it completely. We have no way to see, recover, or be asked to delete this data, because it was never transmitted anywhere in the first place.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand overall traffic — which pages are visited, roughly how many people visit, and general technical details like browser and device type. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to do this, and may collect your IP address and browsing behavior on this site. This data is aggregated and used to improve the site; it is not linked to the values you enter into any calculator.

Google AdSense & advertising

This site may display ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized ads generally at aboutads.info. Our use of advertising cookies is governed by Google's own privacy policies, which you can read at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Cookies, in short

This site itself does not set cookies to store your calculator inputs or identify you personally. The cookies that do get set come from Google Analytics (traffic measurement) and Google AdSense (advertising), as described above. You can block or delete cookies at any time through your browser's settings; doing so may affect how ads are personalized but won't affect the calculators themselves, since they don't rely on cookies to work.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
