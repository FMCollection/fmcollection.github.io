BAC (Blood Alcohol Content)

Estimates blood alcohol concentration from drinks consumed, body weight, and gender using the Widmark formula. Individual results vary significantly with metabolism, food intake, and other factors. Never use this estimate to decide whether it's safe to drive or do anything requiring sobriety — if you've been drinking, don't drive.
