What is lean body mass?

Lean body mass is your total weight minus fat mass — muscle, bone, organs, and water. This calculator uses the Boer formula, one of the more widely cited estimates when a scan isn't available. It's a useful denominator for setting protein targets, since protein needs track lean mass more closely than total weight.
