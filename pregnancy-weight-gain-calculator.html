How much weight gain is recommended in pregnancy?

The Institute of Medicine (IOM) sets recommended total weight-gain ranges based on your BMI before pregnancy — lower BMI generally means a higher recommended gain, and vice versa. These are population guidelines; your own doctor may adjust them for twins or other individual factors.
